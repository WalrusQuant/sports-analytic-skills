# When a Sports Model Is Good Enough

## Predictive
- beats locked baselines on mean primary metric
- wins on a majority of walk-forward folds
- leakage audit CLEAN
- calibration not catastrophic if quoting probabilities

## Explanatory
- effect direction stable across seasons
- uncertainty reported
- no causal overclaim from observational data

## Not good enough
- one hot season only
- no baseline
- random splits on seasonal games
- illegal features
